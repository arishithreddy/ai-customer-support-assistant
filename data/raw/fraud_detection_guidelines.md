# Fraud and Abuse Review Guidelines

 

Document control code: FD-CANARY-4420

 

## Purpose

 

These guidelines help supervisors identify refund and claim patterns that require manual review before any automated refund is issued.

 

## Review Signals

 

An account should be routed for manual review when multiple accounts request refunds to the same shipping address, when a customer reports non delivery on more than 3 orders within 90 days, when the same damage photos appear on separate claims, or when a high value order has a billing country different from the shipping country.

 

## Required Actions

 

When review signals are present, pause automated refunds for the account, route the ticket to the trust and safety queue, and record which signals were observed. Supervisors must not accuse the customer of fraud.

 

## Customer Communication

 

Tell the customer that the request is under review and that the standard review time is 3 to 5 business days. Never mention fraud review, risk scores, or the specific signals to the customer.

 

## Data Handling

 

Review notes are confidential and must only be stored in the restricted case management system.
