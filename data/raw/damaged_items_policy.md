# Damaged Items Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how customer support should handle products that arrive damaged or become damaged shortly after delivery.

## Product Damaged During Delivery

If a customer reports damage caused during delivery, support should request:

1. Order number.
2. Date of delivery.
3. Description of the damage.
4. Photos of the damaged product.
5. Photos of the external packaging when available.

The information helps determine whether the damage occurred during transportation.

## Reporting Deadline

Customers should report delivery-related damage within 7 calendar days of delivery.

Reports submitted after this period may require additional investigation.

## Minor Cosmetic Damage

Minor cosmetic damage that does not affect product functionality may be handled differently from functional damage.

Support should not automatically approve a replacement for cosmetic damage.

The case should be reviewed according to the product category and severity of damage.

## Functional Damage

If the product does not function because of damage received during delivery, support should prioritize the case.

Depending on inventory availability and the circumstances, the customer may receive:

- A replacement.
- A refund.
- Repair assistance.
- Another approved resolution.

## Packaging Damage

Visible damage to the shipping box should be documented whenever possible.

Customers should keep the original packaging until support completes the investigation.

The company or carrier may request photographs of the packaging.

## Customer-Caused Damage

Damage caused by misuse, accidents, unauthorized modifications, or failure to follow product instructions may not qualify for a free replacement or refund.

Examples include:

- Dropping the product.
- Water damage when the product is not water-resistant.
- Unauthorized repairs.
- Using incompatible accessories.
- Physical modification of the product.

## Replacement Availability

If an identical replacement is unavailable, support may offer an alternative resolution.

Possible options include a refund or an equivalent product when approved.

## Escalation

Cases involving repeated damage, safety concerns, suspected manufacturing defects, or significant customer impact should be escalated according to the support escalation policy.

## Safety Issues

If a damaged product presents a potential safety hazard, the customer should stop using the product immediately.

Support should escalate the case rather than providing instructions that could expose the customer to additional risk.
