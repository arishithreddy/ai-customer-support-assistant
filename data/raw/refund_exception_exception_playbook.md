# Refund Exception Playbook

 

Document control code: RX-CANARY-7731

 

## Approval Limits

 

Support agents may issue standard refunds up to 50 dollars without approval. Supervisors may approve refunds and exceptions up to 500 dollars. Any refund or exception above 500 dollars requires approval from the finance manager.

 

## Late Return Exceptions

 

Supervisors may approve a return up to 15 days beyond the standard 30 day return window for premium customers who have not received a return exception in the previous 12 months. Standard customers may receive a late return exception only if the delay was caused by a verified shipping or company error.

 

## Goodwill Credits

 

For shipments delayed more than 10 business days beyond the estimated delivery date, supervisors may offer store credit of up to 15 percent of the order value. Goodwill credit is limited to one per customer per calendar quarter.

 

## Digital Product Exceptions

 

Digital products are normally non refundable. A supervisor may approve a refund for a digital product if the purchase was made within the last 48 hours and the product has not been downloaded or accessed.

 

## Not Eligible for Exceptions

 

Exceptions must not be granted for items damaged through misuse, gift cards, or accounts currently under risk review.

 

## Documentation

 

Every exception must be recorded on the ticket with the reason, the approved amount, and the approving supervisor ID.
