# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Overview

Customers may request a return or refund for eligible products according to the conditions described in this policy.

## Return Window

Most eligible products can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery.

The return period begins on the date the carrier marks the order as delivered.

Returns requested after 30 days may be rejected unless an exception applies.

## Return Conditions

Products must generally be:

- Unused.
- In their original condition.
- Returned with original packaging when applicable.
- Accompanied by the order number or proof of purchase.

Some products may have additional return requirements.

## Damaged Products

Customers who receive a damaged product should contact customer support within 7 days of delivery.

Customers should provide:

- Order number.
- Description of the damage.
- Photographs showing the damage.
- Photographs of the shipping packaging when relevant.

Support may offer a replacement, refund, or another appropriate resolution.

## Wrong Product Received

If a customer receives a product different from what was ordered, they should contact support within 7 days of delivery.

The customer should provide the order number and a photograph of the received product.

The company may provide a prepaid return label when the incorrect item was shipped by the company.

## Refund Processing

Once an approved return has been received and inspected, the refund is normally initiated within 5 business days.

The time required for the refund to appear in the customer's account depends on the payment provider.

Credit and debit card refunds may take an additional 3–10 business days to appear.

## Original Payment Method

Refunds are normally issued to the original payment method.

Support cannot normally redirect a refund to a different payment method.

## Shipping Charges

Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless the return is caused by an error made by the company or the product arrived damaged.

Return shipping charges may apply for customer-initiated returns.

## Non-Returnable Products

Certain products may not be eligible for return because of their nature or applicable regulations.

Examples may include:

- Personalized products.
- Products marked as final sale.
- Certain hygiene-sensitive products.
- Products that have been used or altered.

The product-specific return conditions shown at purchase take precedence.

## Refund Denials

A refund may be denied when:

- The return is outside the allowed return period.
- The product shows significant use or damage caused by the customer.
- The product is not eligible for return.
- Required return information cannot be verified.

## Refund Status

Customers who have received confirmation that a refund was issued but cannot see it in their account should first check with their payment provider.

If the refund remains missing after 10 business days, support should investigate the transaction.
