# Ticket Handling Standard Operating Procedure

## Priority Levels

High priority applies to lost packages with order value above 100 dollars, customers who have contacted support three or more times about the same issue, and premium customers with an issue unresolved for more than 3 business days. Medium priority applies to damaged items, incorrect items, and warranty claims. Low priority applies to general policy questions and invoice requests.

## Response Time Targets

Premium customers should receive a first response within 4 business hours. Standard customers should receive a first response within 24 business hours. High priority tickets should be updated at least once every business day.

## Required Ticket Fields

Every ticket must include the customer ID, order ID if applicable, issue type, priority, a short summary, troubleshooting already completed, and the requested resolution.
 
## Supervisor Handoff

Agents must hand off a ticket to a supervisor when the customer requests a policy exception, when a refund exceeds the agent approval limit, when an account shows risk review flags, or when the customer mentions a chargeback or legal action.

## Closing Tickets

A ticket may be closed only after the resolution has been communicated to the customer and the customer has confirmed, or after 5 business days without a customer reply following the resolution message.
