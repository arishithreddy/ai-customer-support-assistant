# Order Cancellation Policy

## Overview

Customers may request cancellation of an order before it enters the shipping process.

## Cancellation Before Processing

Orders can generally be cancelled when they have not yet entered fulfillment.

Customers should contact support as soon as possible after placing an order.

## Cancellation After Shipment

Once an order has been handed to the shipping carrier, it generally cannot be cancelled through customer support.

The customer may need to receive the order and then request a return if the product is eligible for return.

## Cancellation Request

Customers should provide:

- Order number.
- Reason for cancellation.
- Contact information associated with the order.

Support should verify the order status before confirming cancellation.

## Cancellation Confirmation

A cancellation is not considered complete until the customer receives a cancellation confirmation.

If the order status is still processing, support should verify whether fulfillment has already started.

## Refund After Cancellation

When a cancellation is approved before shipment, the company will initiate a refund to the original payment method.

Refund processing time depends on the payment provider.

## Partial Cancellation

If an order contains multiple products, some items may be cancelled while others have already entered fulfillment.

Support should verify the status of each item before processing a partial cancellation.

## Customized Products

Customized or personalized products may have limited cancellation options because production may begin shortly after the order is placed.

Customers should contact support immediately if they need to cancel such an order.

## Cancellation Due to Pricing Error

The company may cancel an order when a significant pricing or product information error has occurred.

Customers will be notified when an order is cancelled for this reason.

## Cancellation Due to Inventory

An order may be cancelled if the product becomes unavailable before fulfillment.

If this occurs, the customer will receive a refund for the cancelled item.

## Cancellation and Return Difference

Cancellation applies to orders that have not entered the shipping process.

Return requests apply after the customer has received the product.

These are handled under different procedures.
