# Product Troubleshooting Guide

## Purpose

This guide provides basic troubleshooting steps for common product issues.

Customers should stop troubleshooting and contact support when the product presents a potential safety risk.

## Product Will Not Turn On

Customers should:

1. Confirm that the power source is connected.
2. Check whether the power cable is securely connected.
3. Try a known working power outlet when appropriate.
4. Check whether the device has a physical power switch.
5. Allow the device to charge when it uses a battery.
6. Attempt to restart the device.

If the product still does not power on, support should collect the model number and order information.

## Product Is Not Charging

Customers should:

1. Check the charging cable.
2. Confirm that the correct charger is being used.
3. Inspect the cable for visible damage.
4. Try another compatible power outlet.
5. Restart the product.
6. Allow the device to charge for at least 30 minutes.

Customers should not use a damaged charging cable.

## Product Is Overheating

If a product becomes unusually hot, the customer should stop using it and disconnect it from power when it is safe to do so.

Support should determine whether the issue requires escalation.

Customers should not attempt unauthorized repairs.

## Product Is Running Slowly

Customers should:

- Restart the product.
- Close unnecessary applications or processes.
- Check available storage.
- Install approved software updates.
- Check the network connection if the product depends on internet access.

## Connectivity Problems

For products requiring Wi-Fi or Bluetooth connectivity, customers should:

1. Confirm that the network or Bluetooth connection is enabled.
2. Restart the product.
3. Restart the router when applicable.
4. Move the product closer to the network source.
5. Reconnect the device using the normal pairing process.

## Software Update Problems

Customers should verify that the device has sufficient battery or is connected to power before starting an update.

The customer should not disconnect the device during a firmware update unless instructed by support.

## Product Still Not Working

If basic troubleshooting does not resolve the issue, support should collect:

- Product model.
- Serial number when applicable.
- Order number.
- Description of the issue.
- Troubleshooting steps already attempted.

The case may then be evaluated for warranty service or escalation.

## Safety Warning

Customers should not open, modify, or repair products internally unless specifically authorized and instructed by the manufacturer.

Smoke, burning smells, sparks, exposed electrical components, or unusual battery swelling should be treated as safety-related incidents and escalated immediately.
