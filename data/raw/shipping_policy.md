# Shipping Policy

## Overview

Our shipping policy explains available delivery methods, estimated delivery times, shipping charges, and what customers should do when an order is delayed or appears to be lost.

## Standard Shipping

Standard shipping is available for most products and destinations.

- Estimated delivery time: 5–7 business days.
- Shipping charges depend on the customer's location and order value.
- Orders above $75 qualify for free standard shipping.
- Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.
- Delivery estimates begin after the order has been confirmed and handed to the shipping carrier.

## Express Shipping

Express shipping is available for eligible products and locations.

- Estimated delivery time: 2–3 business days.
- Express shipping charges are calculated during checkout.
- Express shipping is not available for every product or destination.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM local time may be processed on the following business day.

## Order Processing

Orders are normally processed within 1–2 business days.

An order may take longer to process when:

- The product is temporarily unavailable.
- The billing or shipping information requires verification.
- The order contains a customized product.
- There is unusually high order volume.

Customers receive a shipping confirmation email after the order has been handed to the carrier.

## Tracking

Customers can track shipped orders using the tracking number included in the shipping confirmation email.

If tracking information does not appear immediately, customers should wait up to 24 hours after receiving the shipping confirmation email.

## Delayed Orders

If an order has passed its estimated delivery date, customers should first check the carrier tracking information.

If there has been no tracking update for more than 3 business days, the customer should contact customer support.

Support may investigate the shipment and contact the carrier when necessary.

## Lost Shipments

A shipment may be considered potentially lost when the carrier has not provided a tracking update for 7 consecutive business days.

Customers should contact support with their order number.

Support will investigate the shipment before issuing a replacement or refund.

## Incorrect Shipping Address

Customers should contact support as soon as possible if they entered an incorrect shipping address.

Address changes are only possible before the order is handed to the carrier.

After shipment, the customer may need to contact the carrier directly.

## International Shipping

International delivery times vary by destination.

Customers may be responsible for customs duties, import taxes, or other government charges.

These charges are not included in the standard shipping fee unless explicitly stated during checkout.

## Shipping Exceptions

Weather events, carrier disruptions, customs delays, natural disasters, and other events outside the company's control may increase delivery times.

Customer support cannot guarantee a specific delivery date when a shipment is affected by an external carrier or customs delay.
