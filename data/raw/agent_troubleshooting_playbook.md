# Agent Troubleshooting Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook is for support agents only. It contains advanced troubleshooting steps, known product issues, and handling procedures that are not published to customers.

## Verifying Customer Identity

Before discussing order details, agents must confirm the order number and the email address on the account. Agents must never read out full payment card details. Agents must never disclose information about an order that does not belong to the verified customer.

## Device Not Powering On Advanced Steps

 After the customer has completed the basic steps in the public troubleshooting guide, ask the customer to charge the device for at least 30 minutes using the original adapter. Then ask them to hold the power button for 15 seconds to perform a hard reset. If the device still does not power on and the purchase is within the warranty period, create a warranty ticket tagged hardware_failure.

## Known Issues

The SoundWave Bluetooth Speaker (PROD-101) on firmware version 2.1 has a known pairing issue with some Android devices. Agents should guide customers to update to firmware version 2.2 through the companion app. The FitTrack Smart Band (PROD-103) may stop syncing after a mobile app update. Agents should ask customers to uninstall and reinstall the app, then pair the band again.

## Lost Package Handling

If tracking has not updated for more than 7 business days, agents should open a carrier investigation and tag the ticket lost_package. If the order value is above 100 dollars, set the ticket priority to high. 

## Tone Guidelines

Agents should acknowledge the customer's frustration, avoid blaming the carrier or the customer, and always state the next step and expected timeline clearly.
