# Warranty Policy

## Overview

The warranty provides coverage for eligible manufacturing defects under the conditions described below.

## Standard Warranty Period

Eligible products include a standard limited warranty of 12 months from the original purchase date.

The warranty period begins on the purchase date shown on the customer's proof of purchase.

## What the Warranty Covers

The warranty generally covers defects in materials and workmanship that occur during normal use.

Examples may include:

- Product fails to power on despite following normal operating instructions.
- Internal component failure during normal use.
- Manufacturing defects discovered during normal operation.

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover

The warranty does not normally cover damage caused by:

- Accidents.
- Misuse.
- Improper installation.
- Unauthorized modifications.
- Normal cosmetic wear.
- Exposure to conditions outside the product specifications.
- Damage caused by incompatible accessories.

## Warranty Claim

Customers requesting warranty service should provide:

- Order number or proof of purchase.
- Product model.
- Description of the problem.
- Date the problem was first noticed.
- Photographs or videos when relevant.

Support may request additional information during the investigation.

## Warranty Replacement

If a product is confirmed to have a covered defect, the company may repair or replace the product.

Replacement products may be refurbished or equivalent products unless otherwise specified.

## Warranty and Returns

The standard 30-day return policy and the 12-month warranty are separate policies.

A product outside the 30-day return period may still qualify for warranty service if the defect is covered.

## Warranty Transfer

Warranty eligibility is generally tied to the original purchase and may not automatically transfer to another owner.

Customers should contact support when ownership has changed.

## Warranty Expiration

Claims submitted after the warranty period may not qualify for free warranty service.

Support should verify the purchase date before approving warranty-related service.

## Extended Warranty

Some products may have an optional extended warranty.

When an extended warranty was purchased, support should verify the customer's order and the specific terms associated with that warranty.

## Warranty Escalation

Cases involving repeated failures, safety concerns, or disagreements regarding warranty eligibility may be escalated to a senior support representative.
