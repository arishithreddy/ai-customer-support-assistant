# Account and Billing FAQ

## How do I change my account email?

Customers can update their account email from the account settings page.

For security reasons, customers may be asked to verify the new email address.

## I forgot my password

Customers should use the "Forgot Password" option on the sign-in page.

A password reset email will be sent to the registered email address.

Customers should contact support if they no longer have access to their registered email address.

## How do I change my billing address?

The billing address can be updated from the account or checkout settings when the order has not yet been finalized.

Changing the billing address does not automatically change the shipping address of an existing order.

## Why was I charged twice?

Customers who believe they were charged twice should provide the order number and transaction details.

Sometimes a second transaction shown by the bank is a temporary authorization rather than a completed charge.

Support should verify the payment status before issuing a refund.

## Payment Failed

A payment may fail because of:

- Insufficient funds.
- Incorrect card information.
- Expired card.
- Bank restrictions.
- Payment processor issues.
- Security verification requirements.

Customers should verify their payment details and contact their bank when necessary.

## Invoice Request

Customers can request an invoice by providing the order number.

Business customers may need to provide additional billing information.

## Refund Appears on Statement

A refund may appear as a pending transaction before it is fully processed by the payment provider.

Customers should allow the payment provider's normal processing period before contacting support about a missing refund.

## Unauthorized Transaction

If a customer believes an account or payment method has been used without authorization, support should treat the case as a security-related issue.

The customer should immediately contact their financial institution when appropriate and change their account password.

Security-related cases may require escalation.

## Account Deletion

Customers may request deletion of their account.

Account deletion may be subject to legal, financial, or regulatory record-retention requirements.

Support should explain the applicable process before completing the request.

## Multiple Accounts

Customers should avoid creating multiple accounts for the same person unless there is a specific business requirement.

Multiple accounts can make order history, refunds, and loyalty benefits difficult to manage.
